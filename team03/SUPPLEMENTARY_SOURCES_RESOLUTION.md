Single reference for the national-source verification that closes the
sector-gap work. Covers **every** gap country in the register — the ones
resolved with a value, the ones left missing, and the ones still unresolved —
with the exact source, evidence, and solution method for each. Data
consistency, robustness, sensitivity, and reproducibility are at the end.

`Datasets/supplementary_source_register.csv` (both generated by
`team03/build_supplementary_sources.py`), `Datasets/sensitivity_analysis.csv`

## 1. Method and rules

Followed by the supplementary notebook (`Supplementary/Supplementary_sources.ipynb`):

1. Sector-level data only; **final consumption**, not generation or sales, unless a
   sales series is explicitly documented as a proxy.
2. Preserve original categories; **never treat a missing value as zero without
   evidence**.
3. Record the **URL, table, and licence** for every source.
4. Licences: UNSD free (cite); SDES open (décret 2017-638, cite + update date);
   GASTAT open-with-attribution; AGEB/ESDM/SBP/Malaysia-EC = cite or excerpt only,
   do not redistribute the PDFs.

The one invariant everything must preserve (verified 100%, §3):

```
Industry + Transport + Residential + Services/Other == FEC (B25_FEC)
```

A missing sector row usually **re-assigns** electricity between sectors rather than
losing it from the total. The single exception in this dataset is Pakistan
1990–1992, where the published `B25_FEC` itself omits industry (§2.11).

## 2. Per-country resolutions

### 2.1 France — use UNdata (cross-check only)

- **Gap:** none — UNdata code `251` (France-Monaco) is complete. The progress note
  wrongly recorded France as absent by looking up name "France" instead of
  "France-Monaco".
- **Source examined:** SDES *Chiffres clés de l'énergie 2026* (sheet P3-75).
- **Decision:** use UNdata, consistent with every other country.
- **Method:** SDES is kept only as a **climate-adjusted cross-check**. Sector mean
  difference vs UNdata: Industry +0.02%, Residential −0.09%, Services/Other +0.02%,
  Transport −0.03%. Note in the report that the series includes Monaco (<1 TWh).

### 2.2 Germany 1990 — linked AGEB estimate

- **Gap:** UNdata has no `276` (Germany) row for 1990; the series starts 1991.
  Former West/East codes `280/278` return HTTP 404.
- **Source:** AG Energiebilanzen *Evaluation Tables* EBD24e, Tables 6.1–6.6
  (Electricity, PJ), read by pymupdf word coordinates (no extractable table borders).
- **Decision:** accepted; `flag_germany_linked = 1`.
- **Method (linked estimate):** `linked_1990 = AGEB_1990 × (UNdata_1991 ÷ AGEB_1991)`.
  AGEB classifies construction under services while UNdata classifies it under
  industry, so the boundary differs (~±10% in 1991). Applying the ratio:
  - Industry = 207.8 × 1.106 = **229.9** TWh
  - Services/Other = 116.4 × 0.881 = **102.6** TWh
  - Transport = 49 PJ ÷ 3.6 = **13.6** TWh (AGEB 1990 direct)
  - Residential = 422 PJ ÷ 3.6 = **117.2** TWh (AGEB 1990 direct)
  - FEC = sum = **463.3** TWh
  Transport and Residential are taken directly from AGEB 1990 (they agree with
  UNdata within 1% in 1991).

### 2.3 Russia and the ex-Soviet states — not filled

- **Gap:** Russia (and Ukraine, Kazakhstan, …) are missing 1990–1991.
- **Decision:** **not filled**.
- **Method:** these states did not yet exist before the USSR dissolved; the series
  legitimately starts in 1992. This is why the global-trend figure uses a fixed
  sample of 179 countries (present in 1990) as the denominator, and discloses the
  coverage effect in the all-reporting line.

### 2.4 Saudi Arabia — unresolved (no transport category)

- **Gap:** `B40_122` transport is **ABSENT** (all 33 years).
- **Sources examined:** GASTAT *Electrical Energy Statistics* 2021/2023; ECRA
  *Annual Statistical Booklet* (2017, mirror HTTP 403).
- **Decision:** **unresolved**.
- **Method:** neither GASTAT nor ECRA publishes a transport/railway category
  (their categories are residential / commercial / government / industrial /
  agricultural / other). The Haramain high-speed rail (2018) is folded into "other";
  Riyadh Metro opened only late 2024 (outside 1990–2022). Transport is **not set to
  zero and not reallocated**; FEC is unaffected (reconcile check).

### 2.5 Indonesia — HEESI fills (applied)

- **Gap:** `B40_122` transport missing 1990–2007 and 2016.
- **Source:** ESDM *Handbook of Energy & Economic Statistics of Indonesia* (HEESI),
  Table 5.4.1 *Energy Consumption in Transportation Sector*, column Electricity (GWh).
  HEESI 2012 (data 2000–2011), HEESI 2018 final (data 2008–2018).
- **Decision:** accepted, **applied**; `flag_indonesia_heesi = 1`.
- **Method:** the decisive evidence is that **UNdata matches HEESI exactly for
  2008–2014** (81, 111, 89, 88, 108, 129, 155 GWh), proving UNdata's Indonesian
  transport *is* the HEESI series. The pre-2008 and 2016 gaps are therefore the same
  series, filled from HEESI 2012 (2000–2007: 44/49/53/53/55/55/67/85 GWh) and
  HEESI 2018 (2016: 223 GWh). In TWh:
  `2000–07 = 0.044, 0.049, 0.053, 0.053, 0.055, 0.055, 0.067, 0.085` and
  `2016 = 0.223`.
  2016 comes from a slightly different vintage than its UNdata neighbours (from 2015
  UNdata diverges from HEESI), flagged but negligible (~0.2 TWh vs ~294 TWh).
  **1990–1999 remain missing** (no HEESI edition covers them).

### 2.6 Viet Nam — rejected (sales)

- **Gap:** transport missing 1995–2022 (present 1990–94 only, 0.036–0.045 TWh).
- **Source examined:** EVN (Vietnam Electricity) annual reports — commercial
  electricity by component.
- **Decision:** **rejected**.
- **Method:** EVN figures are **sales**, not final consumption, and have no transport
  category (urban rail would sit in "others"). Transport stays missing; first metro
  (Hanoi) opened only 2021.

### 2.7 Thailand — not accessible / rejected

- **Gap:** transport missing 1990–1998.
- **Sources examined:** DEDE *Energy Balance of Thailand*; EPPO electricity tables.
- **Decision:** **not accessible / rejected**.
- **Method:** DEDE's site unreachable; EPPO = sales by tariff class (no transport).
  UNdata reports transport = 0.0 in 1999, matching the BTS Skytrain opening in
  December 1999. 1990–98 stays missing (very likely nil but unsourced).

### 2.8 Egypt — rejected (sales, fiscal years)

- **Gap:** transport missing 1990–2011.
- **Source examined:** EEHC *Annual Report 2018/2019* (sold energy by voltage).
- **Decision:** **rejected**.
- **Method:** EEHC figures are **sales** on **fiscal years**, with no transport
  category (Industries / Agriculture / Utilities / Public lighting / Government /
  Residential / Commercial & Others).

### 2.9 Malaysia — documented missing (NEB 2012)

- **Gap:** transport missing 1990–1996.
- **Source:** Energy Commission (Suruhanjaya Tenaga) *National Energy Balance 2012*,
  Figure 31 *Electricity Consumption by Sectors* (archived copy, Wayback
  `20220725103818`; NEB 2022 covers only 2005+).
- **Decision:** **documented missing** (not set to zero).
- **Method:** NEB 2012 Figure 31 reports transport as "–" (not collected) before
  2006, with the note "From 2006 until 2009 data were collected directly from train
  operators". The first electric urban rail — KTM Komuter (Aug 1995) and STAR LRT
  (Dec 1996) — imply small but unquantified values for 1995–96 and nil before. So
  1990–96 stays missing; the official source itself does not split it out.

### 2.10 UAE — rejected (no transport category)

- **Gap:** transport ABSENT.
- **Sources examined:** FCSC/MoEI; DEWA statistics.
- **Decision:** **rejected**.
- **Method:** no transport category anywhere; DEWA covers Dubai only.

### 2.11 Pakistan — resolved (three sub-issues)

The only gap country where a missing row **understates the national total**.

- **Gap:** Industry missing 1990–1992; Transport nil 2017–2022.
- **Sources:** State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) *Handbook of Statistics* Table 2.3
  (*Electricity, GWh*, FY 1971-72 → 2019-20); NEPRA *State of Industry Report 2021*;
  Pakistan Railways *Yearbook 2023-24* (Fuel consumption).

**(a) Industry 1990–92 + FEC recompute.** UNdata's published `B25_FEC`
(18.45 / 20.30 / 21.59) equals only the three reported sectors — the total itself
omits industry. Filling industry and recomputing FEC:

| Year | UN FEC | SBP Industry | Recomputed FEC | SBP Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1990 | 18.45 | 10.3 | 28.75 | 28.77 |
| 1991 | 20.30 | 11.2 | 31.50 | 31.53 |
| 1992 | 21.59 | 12.3 | 33.89 | 33.88 |

This fixes the fake 1992→1993 jump (+69% → +7.7%). `flag_fec_recomputed = 1`.
(Filling industry without recomputing FEC would break the sector-sum invariant by
55–57%.)

**(b) Transport 2017–19 = 0 (reported nil).** SBP Table 2.3 records traction "–" for
FY 2017-18 / 2018-19 / 2019-20. The 2019-20 row's *other* components do not
reconcile to its total (−10,001 GWh, a source-level inconsistency), but the traction
cell is unambiguously nil and the Railways yearbook independently confirms nil
electric energy for that year. `flag_reported_nil = 1`.

**(c) Transport 2020–22 = 0 (sourced).** The Pakistan Railways *Yearbook 2023-24*
"Fuel consumption" table records **electric energy = Nil** for FY 2020-21, 2021-22,
2022-23 (electric-locomotive service suspended, nil electric locomotives). This
makes 2020–22 a sourced value, not a convention.

**Year convention** (must be respected in any Pakistan source work): UNdata year *Y*
= SBP fiscal year *(Y−1)–Y* up to 2001, then *Y–(Y+1)* from 2002; FY 2001-02 is
absent; 2017 and 2019–2023 have no exact SBP match (revised/newer UNdata figures).

### 2.12 Iran — not accessible (Persian only)

- **Gap:** transport missing 1990–1998 (series starts 1999 at 0.011 TWh, rising to
  0.66 TWh by 2022).
- **Source:** Ministry of Energy energy balance.
- **Decision:** **not accessible**.
- **Method:** sites unreachable and Persian only. Tehran Metro Line 1 opened 1999
  (exactly when the series starts), which suggests 1990–98 is ~nil, but without an
  accessible source it is left missing, not set to zero.

### 2.13 Bangladesh — assessed (diesel railway; metro Dec 2022)

- **Gap:** transport missing 1990–2020 (31 years). UNdata itself reports
  2021 = 0.465 TWh and 2022 = 0.498 TWh (kept as-is).
- **Source:** Bangladesh Railway; Dhaka MRT Line-6 (first electric urban rail,
  opened 28 Dec 2022).
- **Decision:** **assessed**; 1990–2020 left missing.
- **Method:** Bangladesh Railway is diesel-hauled, so transport was nil in practice
  before the metro. The 2021 value (0.465 TWh) slightly precedes the documented
  metro opening — noted as an observation only, not altered.

## 3. Data consistency — the invariant

```
Industry + Transport + Residential + Services/Other == FEC  (within 1%)
```

- Verified **6,932 / 6,932** country-years (100%).
- `B47_123` (the parent aggregate) is **never** added — present in the raw CSV is
  False, so there is no double counting.
- `Services_Other` is the row-wise sum of `B48_1232 + B49_1235 + B51_1234`
  (`min_count=1`); recomputed vs stored = **max |diff| 0.0000 TWh**.
- After every fill, re-run `validate_dataset.py`; the invariant holds (the fills
  only change a sector, never break the sum).

## 7. Technical explanations (report / PPT)

* **Unit.** SDMX `UNIT` reads `HSO` but `OBS_VALUE` is **TJ**; `TWh = OBS_VALUE / 3600`.
* **Sectors.** Industry `B26_121`, Transport `B40_122`, Residential `B50_1231`,
  Services/Other = `B48_1232 + B49_1235 + B51_1234`; never add `B47_123`.
* **Combined codes.** Data-bearing codes are `251` (France-Monaco), `382`
  (Italy-San Marino), `579` (Norway-Svalbard), `757` (Switzerland-Liechtenstein);
  `250/380/578/756` return 404. Choropleth keys on `UN_A3` with `251→250` etc.
* **Fixed sample.** 179 countries present in 1990 (Germany added back by the linked
  estimate). Ex-Soviet states appear only from 1992.
* **Generation ≠ consumption.** 28,918 TWh generation vs 24,936 TWh final
  consumption (gap ~14% = losses + own-use + trade); generation mix is background,
  never part of the four sector shares.
* **Associations, not causes.** The Kaya decomposition
  (E = P × (GDP/P) × (E/GDP)) is arithmetic and observational; it describes *what*
  accounts for growth, never *why*.
